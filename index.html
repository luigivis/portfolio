Backend, end to end.

Information Systems Engineer with 6+ years designing and operating distributed systems in regulated and high-throughput environments — banking, adtech and logistics. My work tends to live where correctness and cost meet: event-driven pipelines, caching layers, and APIs that have to stay up.
